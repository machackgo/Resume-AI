# Future curriculum and product boundary (documentation only)

Stage 4B proves training mechanics on immutable Stage 4A evidence-reasoning data. It does not teach application generation, optimize benchmark quality, merge a model, or authorize Stage 4C execution.

Any later professional curriculum must be separately versioned. Evidence classification alone is insufficient. Future tasks should cover explicit job requirements, required/preferred distinctions, responsibilities, technologies, experience and exact numeric requirements; evidence-to-job mapping and strongest relevant evidence; missing information, adjacent-technology distinctions and action-strength calibration; ATS-oriented resume summaries, skills and accomplishment bullets; professional cover letters; grounded semantic application answers with USER_INPUT_REQUIRED for missing facts; personalized recruiter/outreach drafts; explanations of candidate-job fit; and structured plans for downstream agents.

Priority: TRUTH / EVIDENCE → JOB RELEVANCE → ATS COMPATIBILITY → PROFESSIONAL WRITING. Never sacrifice truth for keyword matching. There is no universal ATS score. Evaluate explicit measurable criteria: parseable standard section headings, clean single-column-friendly structure where appropriate, relevant terminology, grounded action/result bullets, concise accomplishments, consistent titles/dates, appropriate skills, professional tone and job-specific tailoring. Avoid unsupported keyword stuffing, invented technologies, metrics or experience. Avoid tables/text boxes when the chosen output format makes them risky for ATS.

Future cover letters should have a job-specific opening, concise candidate-role connection, strongest relevant evidence, company/role alignment supported only by available context, professional tone, no generic filler or unsupported claims/company facts, and a concise closing. Outreach inputs should be the job, relevant professional contact and candidate RAG evidence; outputs should be concise personalized drafts. Do not invent relationships or contact details, and do not spam. None of these examples are to be created or trained in Stage 4B.

Qwen is the reasoning/generation brain. An orchestrator will connect separate job-search/discovery and filtering tools, browser navigation/upload tools, PDF/DOCX resume and cover-letter rendering, application-state storage, and candidate RAG memory. A later outreach agent may use professional-person search/LinkedIn-compatible tools and public professional contact discovery to identify relevant recruiters, hiring managers or team members, then supply job + person + RAG context to Qwen for LinkedIn/email drafts and review/send. Qwen itself is not the browser, search engine, vector database, renderer, persistent scheduler, 24/7 worker, LinkedIn/search connector or application-state database.

Future user-selected modes may prepare 1, 10, 20 or 30 applications, or search continuously until stopped. These are orchestration features. Continuous production operation should use persistent cloud workers/agent infrastructure and should not require the user's laptop to stay awake.

Exact boundary: after the Stage 4B report and readiness decision, STOP. A future separately authorized Stage 4C may select a full-training configuration. No Stage 4C training, ATS/cover-letter dataset generation, full QLoRA, model merging, browser/job/application/outreach agents, continuous operation or production deployment occurs in this stage.
