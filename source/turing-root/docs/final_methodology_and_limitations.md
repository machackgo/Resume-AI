# Stage 4C-0 methodology and limitations

This document supplies the narrative basis for the final Stage 4C-0 report. Final attempted, accepted, rejected, revised, split, token, test, protection and release figures must come from the parent pipeline's final machine-readable audits. Intermediate batch summaries are diagnostic records, not final release totals. An input or factory freeze is distinct from a curriculum release, and neither is authorization to train.

The work is confined to professional-domain research, curriculum contracts, original synthetic inputs, teacher demonstrations, independent-pass criticism, validation and future experiment design. It performs no QLoRA training, optimizer steps, adapter merging, model-weight modification, production deployment, browser implementation or agent implementation. The frozen Stage 4A dataset is referenced rather than rewritten. Frozen RAG remains the candidate-evidence boundary.

## Research methodology, provenance and evidence strength

The research snapshot, verified on 2026-09-29, covers the ten requested areas: ATS parsing; professional resume writing; technical/AI resumes; job interpretation; cover letters; application answers; outreach; truthfulness; current conventions; and accessibility. Its source register contains 30 public sources and 65 original normalized principles. The search favored primary ATS documentation, university career services, employer guidance, professional ethics, government application guidance and first-party research summaries. It excluded anonymous advice, SEO resume aggregators, private candidate records, paywalled corpora and mass collection of job postings or contact profiles.

Each source records a URL, publisher, verification date, supported publication/update date where available, access method, confidence, scope, limitations, licensing disposition and linked principle IDs. Publication dates are not guessed from crawl dates. Most evidence was inspected through indexed public page text returned by web search; a smaller number of pages were opened directly. Failed or restricted direct fetches were not bypassed. The snapshot stores original notes and paraphrased principles, not a complete archive of source HTML or copied resume examples. The source manifest makes this access limitation explicit. See [research report](../research/stage4c0/research_report.md), [sources](../research/stage4c0/sources.jsonl) and [research manifest](../research/stage4c0/research_manifest.json).

Sources support different kinds of conclusions. Vendor documentation describes that vendor's extraction behavior. Career-center guidance supplies professional conventions without demonstrating that one wording causes better hiring outcomes. Public survey summaries describe their respondent populations, not every employer or senior technical role. W3C guidance concerns accessibility and reading order, which are related to but distinct from ATS parsing. Government guidance is destination- and jurisdiction-specific. The normalized principles therefore distinguish source-supported advice, conservative inference, research-informed product rules and explicit user requirements contextualized by research.

The research supports clear technical contributions, truthful qualifications, selective relevance and readable structure. It does not establish the optimal task mixture, dataset size, teacher configuration, number of revisions, sequence length or training order. Those are engineering choices and proposed experiments. The evidence base is weighted toward US/UK university and early-career contexts; senior industry, international and academic-CV conventions are less comprehensively represented.

No public resume corpus, long copied job-posting corpus, private application history, real personal contact details or unclear-license external training dataset is included. Public posting structure informed original synthetic scenarios. No independently curated real-world job evaluation corpus was released by this research work. A future corpus would need posting identity, dated provenance, permitted use and redistribution review before inclusion.

## Normalized professional principles and conflicting guidance

ATS-oriented defaults are conventional section names, logical order, explicit text, predictable identity/date fields, supported skills and consistent terminology. The design does not define a universal ATS score, promise a ranking outcome or claim that one successful parser implies compatibility with every ATS. [Greenhouse's parsing guidance](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse) and [Lever's developer documentation](https://hire.lever.co/developer/documentation) are evidence for product-specific parser concerns, not a universal scoring model.

Resume writing prioritizes an actual contribution, relevant technical detail and a supported result or purpose. Metrics are useful when evidence supplies them; their absence calls for a precise qualitative statement. Stronger prose must not strengthen factual ownership, scale, seniority or impact. Projects remain projects, API consumption remains consumption, and studied methods remain distinct from implemented systems. Whole-resume review checks chronology, section allocation, repetition and relevance as well as individual bullets. A summary is optional and must earn its space through specific positioning. The technical-writing principles draw on [MIT resume guidance](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes/) and [UT Austin technical bullets](https://careerservices.cns.utexas.edu/resources/resumes/strong-bullets-technical-resumes); exact grounding constraints are also explicit product requirements.

Job analysis preserves requirements, preferences, responsibilities, eligibility, employment conditions, exclusions, exact numbers and logical alternatives. A broad cloud preference does not establish an AWS requirement. No candidate fit can be inferred from a job statement alone. Cover letters connect selected evidence to the role without repeating the entire resume or inventing company mission, culture or personal attachment. Application answers address the actual question and its explicit limit. Behavioral structure is useful when the evidence supports a relevant situation and action; it is not required for a simple factual response. Outreach uses supplied contact relevance and a proportionate request, without invented familiarity, referral or recipient authority.

Several professional conventions are context-dependent:

- **Length:** student one-page advice does not establish a universal rule for experienced candidates, academic CVs or destination-specific applications. Employer instructions take priority. The research snapshot records current USAJOBS limits as time-sensitive federal guidance, not a general resume rule.
- **Quantification:** advice to quantify does not permit estimates to become fabricated outcomes. Exact grounded numbers or qualitative contributions satisfy this product's truth hierarchy.
- **Technical detail:** specificity should explain contribution and purpose; jargon density and keyword repetition are not objectives.
- **Layout and accessibility:** correctly tagged columns can have accessible reading order, while some parsers document complex layouts as risks. Simple ordered content is a conservative default. JSON validation cannot certify a rendered document's PDF tags, reading order or assistive-technology behavior. [W3C reading-order guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Techniques/pdf/PDF3) informs the renderer boundary.
- **AI authorship:** institutional cautions about authenticity and review remain relevant even though the product generates drafts. Grounding, reviewable provenance and employer-specific AI restrictions take precedence over generic productivity claims.
- **Outreach purpose:** informational contact and an application follow-up are different requests. The supplied purpose controls the draft; one networking template is not universal.

Truth, user constraints and relevant evidence outrank style, keyword alignment and concision. This ordering is a normative product contract, supported by professional ethics, rather than an empirical claim that career-center articles define a complete grounding algorithm.

## Stable tasks and observable functional supervision

The interface retains ten task discriminators. Section-specific variants share one task with a section enum, keeping the vocabulary small while supporting whole artifacts and local transformations.

| Task | Observable transformation | Structured result |
|---|---|---|
| `ANALYZE_JOB` | Raw JD → source-spanned atoms → required/preferred and logical distinctions → entities, exact numbers and ambiguity | Typed job analysis; no pre-extracted answers supplied for raw-analysis examples |
| `MAP_JOB_TO_CANDIDATE` | Requirements → scoped evidence → support labels → supported claims and gaps | Requirement-level evidence map |
| `SELECT_RESUME_CONTENT` | Job priorities → supported events → relevance and redundancy selection → section allocation | Ordered content plan with explicit selections and omissions |
| `GENERATE_RESUME` | Job/evidence context → supported selection → coherent sections → professional content → grounding audit | Complete structured resume for the scoped candidate |
| `GENERATE_RESUME_SECTION` | Requested section → relevant supported facts → section-specific composition | Typed summary, skills, experience, projects, education or certifications section |
| `REWRITE_BULLET_FOR_JOB` | Original grounded content → relevant emphasis → precise wording without factual escalation | One grounded bullet |
| `GENERATE_COVER_LETTER` | Role understanding → selected examples → supplied company/role connection → concise narrative | Salutation, paragraphs, closing and signature |
| `ANSWER_APPLICATION_QUESTION` | Question classification → exact facts, semantic evidence or missing personal decision → constrained answer | Grounded answer or `USER_INPUT_REQUIRED` |
| `IDENTIFY_MISSING_INFORMATION` | Requested action → material prerequisites → absent or conflicting facts/preferences | Specific missing fields and actionable questions |
| `DRAFT_OUTREACH` | Supplied contact relevance → limited candidate evidence → purpose-appropriate request | Structured outreach draft for review |

All task outputs have version, task and status fields, a task-specific artifact, and a compact observable trace. Trace fields record selected requirements, selected/excluded evidence, applicable principles and bounded decision codes. They do not contain hidden chain-of-thought, private reasoning transcripts or unbounded self-explanation. Selected evidence and content plans are application-level audit artifacts.

Candidate-facing factual text carries fact/evidence references alongside its text. This includes identities, skills, dates, titles, credentials and paragraphs, not only resume bullets. Those references remain application metadata rather than citations printed into a resume. Pure courtesy or an ordinary nonfactual invitation may have empty references only when independently reviewed as nonfactual. Generic claims of ability, a recipient's identity and supposed relationships are factual even without numbers or technical nouns.

The retained approach is a mixture: concise traces accompany composed artifacts, while separate mapping, content-selection, section and rewrite examples teach focused operations. It avoids duplicating a long analysis transcript inside every artifact and retains complete resumes to teach cross-section coherence. Actual token reports determine whether the proposed mixture fits the demonstrated training envelope; longer intermediate text is not assumed to improve learning.

## Candidate/job factory and ground-truth boundary

Synthetic ground truth is created before professional targets. Candidate facts have stable IDs, candidate ownership, event scope, exact values and explicit provenance. Evidence records retain source IDs, content spans and verification scope. The hidden oracle adds expected labels, relevant evidence whitelists and status prerequisites for deterministic evaluation; it is excluded from teacher, critic and future student messages. Hidden annotations cannot license a fact absent from the visible context.

The v2 input catalog preserves the original technical scenarios and records parent hashes while diversifying qualifications and career scaffolding at the generator level. It includes degree-free paths and non-degree credentials, different academic disciplines, graduate research, role/date variation and explicit project origins. Counterfactual technical variants remain in one split. Added families address explicit negative experience, supplied motivation, trusted versus missing authorization/availability/work preferences and selection from a larger bounded history. The input freeze is documented in [catalog coverage](catalog_v2_coverage.md) and [input audit](catalog_v2_input_audit.json).

These are authored scenario choices, not a sampled applicant population. A broad variety of degree labels does not by itself establish realistic career distributions. Scenario realism still depends on coherent chronology, plausible role transitions, explicit project context and evidence that actually supports each contribution. No model output was used to retroactively create candidate evidence.

Jobs are original reconstructions of researched structures, with role, responsibility, qualification and condition variation. They are not copied postings. Some renderer branches remain shared across several train families, and the JSON/profile skeleton is necessarily common. Distinct style labels are not counted as proof of distinct prose templates. Lexical shingle checks complement substantive family isolation, but neither proves semantic independence.

Grouped splits keep candidate, job, counterfactual, derived-task and substantive-family lineages together. This avoids presenting one technical counterfactual in train and its answer-bearing twin in test. Split proportions and task counts are descriptive; related task views of one candidate are not independent samples. Small per-task held-out counts and some newly added train-only behaviors limit generalization claims. No coverage-saturation claim is justified.

## Frozen RAG compatibility: what the bridge establishes

Professional examples use a bounded projection of evidence compatible with the frozen context contract: candidate/event/source identifiers, untrusted content, exact citation handles, provenance and separately scoped authenticated attestations. The cap is five distinct content groups with each content string at most 640 characters. Multiple corroborating source entries may refer to an exact-text group. Retrieval membership and an authenticated-looking source label do not by themselves prove a claim.

The versioned bridge translates known synthetic origin aliases to frozen canonical values and records inverse sidecars. Source identity, event scope, spans and exact support policy must survive the round trip. A v2 repair explicitly maps the authored synthetic-history origin to the frozen synthetic-fixture origin without promoting trust. Unknown aliases still reject. Compatibility claims must cite the recorded CPU checks against real frozen helpers.

This is a context/helper integration test using authored synthetic evidence. It does not establish end-to-end retrieval quality, run a new embedding experiment, change chunking or reranking, retrieve from the user's private documents, or prove that the model will select correct evidence from a live vector store. The MiniLM/vector/candidate-isolation architecture remains frozen. Future model comparisons must use the same frozen RAG context policy so that retrieval changes do not confound adapter effects.

## Teacher, critic and revision architecture

The requested expert configuration is `gpt-6-astra` with `ultra` reasoning. Runtime backend identity and actual reasoning configuration are not independently exposed by this orchestration and must not be asserted as observed facts. Requested configuration, prompt versions, request hashes, raw response hashes, seed, rubric/schema versions, critic bindings and revision lineage are recorded. Reproducibility means reconstructing scenarios, requests and validation, not promising bit-identical stochastic model responses.

The teacher receives visible task input, relevant normalized principles, task rubric, output schema and declared constraints. A fresh critic receives visible evidence, proposed output, exact hashes and the auditable-leaf inventory. It does not receive teacher hidden reasoning or oracle answers. The critic checks every substantive leaf and its factual atoms, including implications that simple keyword matching may miss. Its review is bound to the exact input and artifact; a changed artifact needs a fresh valid review.

The generator and critics use the **same requested Astra model family**. Their separate contexts and roles improve procedural separation but are not independent model-family judgments. Correlated blind spots, shared stylistic preferences and anchoring remain possible. Deterministic checks provide a different method of control, but they do not turn model judgments into human expert certification. **No actual human professional-quality audit was performed.** Any representative inspection or spot review performed by an agent must be reported as model-assisted review, and a prepared manual-inspection sample must not be described as completed human validation.

The bounded policy allows one initial attempt and at most two revisions, each preserving prior outputs and obtaining fresh criticism and validation. Teacher self-confidence and self-scores are not acceptance evidence. Failures are retained rather than regenerated indefinitely. Malformed responses count as failures. Exact attempted/revised/rejected denominators belong in the final metrics, including interrupted requests where measurable. Provider-side token billing and monetary costs remain unknown unless exposed; measured Qwen training-format tokens must not be relabeled as teacher usage.

Teacher.v2 is an additive routing clarification: UNKNOWN mapping rows contain no supporting references, and material missing factual/preferences route to `USER_INPUT_REQUIRED`. It does not change truth thresholds or candidate facts. Factory v2 is a separately recorded implementation snapshot superseding v1 after systemic validator/bridge defects were found in preflight. Historical manifests, source archives, raw responses and prior request bytes remain preserved; saved outputs require revalidation under the current checker. This versioning is more precise than claiming that no implementation change occurred during development.

## Hard gates, professional rubric and deterministic limits

Hard failures reject a record regardless of prose quality: malformed schemas; unknown or cross-candidate references; unsupported factual atoms; numeric, technology, action-strength, identity, credential or date mismatches; invalid event scope; fabricated employers or roles; unsupported personal/legal answers; violated explicit constraints; missing required audit coverage; stale hashes; and complete sequences above the registered envelope. Grounding requires the maximum rubric rating. Other dimensions require at least 3 on the 0–4 scale and an unweighted mean of at least 3.5, plus unconditional critic acceptance with no unresolved issues. The frozen [acceptance gates](../contracts/acceptance_gates.v1.json) are the source of these thresholds.

Soft quality issues include vague contribution wording, weak ordering, generic openings, avoidable repetition, unsupported stylistic superlatives, excessive length when no explicit limit is violated, and poor selection despite available relevant evidence. These may justify bounded revision. A stylistic weakness becomes a hard truth problem when it changes what the candidate did, implies leadership or production scale, invents a company fact, or strengthens an assertion into verification. An explicit word/character limit is an enforceable user constraint, not a discretionary style preference.

The validator independently traverses actual artifact content instead of trusting a teacher-supplied claim list. Exact source values, visible technology tokens, numeric strings, actions, entities and event relationships are checked. The semantic critic separately assesses entailment, missing predicates, unsupported capabilities and the factual/nonfactual distinction. Deterministic mismatches cannot be overridden by semantic similarity or good prose. Positive controls are necessary because a checker that rejects every output would not establish useful grounding.

The v2 repairs remain bounded: exact non-degree credential titles can appear as education/training without becoming degrees; an explicit instrument adjunct under the same supported action is distinguished from a new action claim; an education noun phrase is distinguished from model training; narrowly nonfactual invitations remain subject to independent review. These repairs address recorded false positives and do not authorize new technologies, metrics, leadership, dates or credentials. See [change record](validator_v2_change_record.md), [action disambiguation](validator_v2_action_disambiguation.md) and [final implementation scope](validator_v2_final_scope.md).

Lexical rules are not a universal entailment engine. They can miss paraphrased implication and can reject valid wording; the critic can also err. Zero known accepted hard failures means no failure was identified by the stated finite checks and reviews. It is not proof that every possible claim interpretation is correct.

## Known oracle defects and challenge-status ambiguity

The preauthored oracle is also fallible. Recorded mapping quarantines include ambiguous decomposition of possession versus verified credentials, compound operational/recovery requirements, overbroad customer-workflow support and disputed hybrid-search terminology. A further documented whitelist defect enumerates implementation evidence even though the requirement permits evaluation **or** implementation; the independent teacher and critic selected a valid evaluation alternative. Such a whitelist is incomplete, not a reason to force the teacher toward the enumerated answer.

These cases remain quarantined with frozen source facts and outputs preserved. They are excluded from SFT and from preference pairs when the proposed rejection reason would incorrectly label a defensible answer as inferior. Other tasks in the same family require their own independent checks; family membership does not confer acceptance. The final report should distinguish confirmed teacher grounding failures from schema/routing failures, evaluator false positives and disputed scenario labels. A raw rejection rate is not automatically a hallucination rate. See [mapping dispositions](../work/full-v1/quarantine-dispositions-v1.json) and [OR-whitelist dispositions](../work/full-v1-revision1/train-01/quarantine-dispositions-v1.json).

The external challenge is an isolated **synthetic** holdout, not a real-world or separately human-authored benchmark. Its frozen inputs include cases where an expected `COMPLETE` status conflicts with conservative missing-information handling or absent mandatory project-origin information. The disposition preserves all challenge inputs while separating scored references from disputed, unscored references. No teacher revision was requested to force those disputed cases into the expected status. See [challenge dispositions](../work/full-v1/challenge/quarantine-dispositions-v1.json).

This partial reference coverage limits the challenge's usefulness. Unscored cases must remain visible in the report; they cannot disappear from denominators without explanation or become implied successes. Newly corrected challenge cases would need a separately versioned, independently authored evaluation design rather than tuning the current dataset against the frozen challenge. Final challenge counts and release eligibility belong to the final audit. The existence of isolated inputs does not imply that every reference is sound.

## Future training and evaluation design — not executed

The minimum proposed comparison retains Base Qwen plus frozen RAG as a control and compares two separately trained adapters from the same frozen base: Stage 4A evidence-only and interleaved Stage 4A plus professional curriculum. The Stage 4B smoke adapter is an engineering artifact, not an evidence-trained scientific baseline. Professional-only and staged-training arms are optional later ablations, not required to begin identifying grounding regression.

The proposed initial mixture is 50% Stage 4A and 50% professional **assistant-target tokens**, with frozen datasets referenced independently. This is a conservative experimental choice, not a measured optimum. Professional token weights are proposed as resume 20%, semantic application answers 15%, mapping 15%, analysis 10%, selection 10%, sections 10%, cover letters 8%, missing-information routing 7%, rewrite 3% and outreach 2%. Final accepted task/token coverage must be checked before using these weights. A small seed set should not be repeatedly replayed to achieve a prestigious step count; the proposed first experiment is capped at one professional pass-equivalent with a matched evidence-only supervised-token budget. See [future experiment design](future_training_and_evaluation.md).

The real local Qwen tokenizer and exact intended chat template determine input, target and complete-conversation lengths. System, user and RAG tokens are masked; the structured assistant target and its end marker receive SFT loss. Hidden oracle, critiques, model receipts and private reasoning are excluded. Thinking is disabled in the proposed production-aligned serialization. Teacher prompt size and student training sequence size are distinct measurements.

The inspected engineering history supports complete optimizer steps at 4,233 tokens and a forward/backward tensor demonstration at 4,608. It does not establish complete all-active optimization at 4,608. The curriculum uses 4,233 as the conservative provisional ceiling. Overflow requires coherent revision or task decomposition with fresh critique and tokenization, never truncation. Section and selection tasks supplement whole resumes rather than replacing whole-document coherence. Final observed sequence statistics must be inserted from the exact-token audit, not estimated from characters or copied from intermediate batches.

A future professional checkpoint is rejected for an observed critical violation on the registered adversarial suite, including invented metrics, technology substitution, action inflation, cross-candidate evidence, unsupported personal facts, injection obedience or fabricated tool execution. Evidence accuracy, UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED confusion, citation entailment, numeric fidelity and technology fidelity are separately evaluated against the evidence-only arm and unchanged base control. The proposed paired macro-accuracy gate allows no more than a two-percentage-point loss; uncertainty that cannot exclude a material five-point loss requires more separately authored evaluation, not relaxed thresholds. Finite-suite perfection is not a universal reliability claim.

Professional evaluation reports task-level denominators and uncertainty for grounding, hallucinated claims, numeric/technology fidelity, supported requirement coverage, supported keyword alignment, evidence utilization, selection, writing, concision, redundancy, ATS structure, letter specificity, answer grounding, correct abstention, outreach personalization and schema validity. Unsupported JD requirements are not counted as content the candidate should have included. Blinded judgments and eventual human review would strengthen evaluation beyond the current same-family critics. No opaque aggregate resume score should conceal a truth failure.

Rejected artifacts remain a separate research record with structured reasons. A future preference pair needs a genuinely inferior rejected artifact and a valid accepted alternative for the same input. Oracle disputes and ambiguous challenge statuses do not supply reliable preference labels. No DPO, reward model or preference optimization is performed in this stage.

## Product and release boundaries

Qwen performs job/evidence reasoning and professional composition. The future orchestrator owns workflow state, cancellation, retries and run modes of one, ten, twenty, thirty or continuous-until-stopped applications. Deterministic application code fills exact profile fields; the database stores preferences; the File Generator renders PDF/DOCX; job/browser/outreach agents discover jobs or people, navigate, upload and execute authorized actions. Qwen must not claim it clicked, uploaded, submitted or sent anything.

The documented future flow is job discovery → job analysis → evidence mapping → content selection → resume/optional letter/application answers → deterministic rendering → browser workflow → stop/review policy before submission → optional selected-contact outreach draft → review/send policy → saved application state. These are interface boundaries only, not implemented agents.

A final Stage 4C-0 decision must rest on the release validator, immutable lineage, protected-artifact verification, historical tests, split/leakage audits, token reports and accepted professional quality. Small size is not a failure by itself, and a large row count is not evidence of readiness. The remaining limitations are concrete: synthetic and US/UK-weighted coverage, repeated render/profile structures, small clustered holdouts, incomplete oracle whitelists, partly unscored challenge references, same-family teacher/critic dependence, no actual human audit, and no real-world parser or hiring-outcome validation. The parent report must state how these limit the recommended next experiment and whether any blocks the release gates. This narrative does not substitute for that decision or authorize the next stage.
